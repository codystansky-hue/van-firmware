Van hub — build card (ESP32-C3 SuperMini), firmware v1, box v1

Replaces the Uno on the flow meter. Victron, Renogy and the MaxxFan are wireless, so the hub has two connections: power (the converter's USB-C plug) and the flow meter on a JST-SM plug. Everything else lives in one printed box: the ESP and the 10k / 20k divider. Sensors plug and unplug at the JST-SM, a hand's width outside the box; no headers needed (header pins and DuPont plugs shake loose in a van, the JST-SM latches). Box: S:\aiCAD\van_hub_box. Firmware and setup: S:\van-firmware\hub\README.md.

The lump (inside the box) and the plug

The meter's yellow wire puts out 5 V pulses; the ESP takes 3.3 V. The 10k + 20k divider sits in the box's bay, between the ESP wires and the pigtail, so any 3-wire sensor plugs straight in and a new meter needs no parts.

Red / green / white is the LED-strip order (5V / data / GND); check your pigtail, some kits swap the colours. Leave alone: 3V3, G2 (boot pin), G8 (on-board LED), G9 (BOOT button).

Build

  1. Print the box (box above).
  2. Flash it on the bench first. ESP into the PC with a USB-C data cable, say “flash the hub”. It comes up as the VanHub Wi-Fi: join it, open http://192.168.4.1, Wi-Fi at the bottom: pick the Starlink network, password, save + join. Then http://van.local works from the phone (README section 2).
  3. Pad wires: three ~6 cm thin wires into the 5V, GND and G10 pad holes from underneath, soldered on top, nothing standing proud on top. They leave the board along its underside: the box floor has a 5 mm channel under each long edge.
  4. The lump (drawing above): G10 wire → joint of the 10k and the 20k; the 20k's other end spliced into the GND run; the 10k's other end → pigtail green. 5V wire → pigtail red, GND run → pigtail white. Heat-shrink each joint, then one sleeve over the lot (about 22 × 8 mm).
  5. Checks (unpowered): at the pigtail plug: red–white not shorted; green–white ≈ 30 k (10k + 20k).
  6. Into the box: board chip side up, about 1 mm back from the USB end; lower it onto the pedestal, slide it toward the end wall until the USB-C sits in the slot. Lump in the bay; pigtail over the bridge, zip tie through the bridge's tunnel and around it, out of the window.
  7. Lid: its tongue drops into the cable notch, its stop bar behind the board. Two M3 × 10 into the lugs: snug, not hard (PETG threads strip).
  8. Meter half: the other pigtail half straight onto the meter: red–red, white–black, green–yellow, solder + heat-shrink. Plug it in.
  9. Mount and power: ears or VHB; converter on the fused 12 V, its USB-C plug into the slot. The blue LED blinks every 2 s (through the lid only if it's printed in a light colour).

Bring-up (http://van.local)

  1. Water: run a tap: the Water card shows gal/min and counts up. No flow at all while water runs: the meter is a bare open collector and the 20k holds G10 low; open the lid and snip one leg of the 20k (the ESP's own pull-up then does the job). Once it counts: fill a 1 gal jug, compare, and set pulses per litre on the setup page (default 450). Fill the tank, press Tank filled.
  2. Victron (once per device: 200W Aft, 320W Fwd, Jimbo): VictronConnect → the device → gear → ⋮ → Product info → Instant readout details: Show, copy the key with the copy button. http://van.local/setup (user van, password vanhub-ota) → Victron: paste it into that device, give it its name, Save. The line turns green, key OK.
  3. Renogy: nothing to set. The Battery card shows the BMS line within a minute; if not, close the DC Home app (the battery takes one connection at a time).
  4. MaxxFan: shows up by itself when both are on the Starlink Wi-Fi (setup page address maxxfan).
  5. Where to mount it: the setup page shows each Victron device and the Renogy with its signal. Better than about −85 dBm is fine; if one keeps going “old”, move the hub toward it or out of any metal cabinet.
  6. Passwords: bottom of the setup page, replace the defaults.

2026-10-12, hub firmware v1, box v1: flow on G10 with the pull-up on, rising edges, 450 pulses per litre, 25 gal tank (both on the setup page). The window takes a second pigtail and the bay has room for the tank probe's parts later.
